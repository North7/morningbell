How to read Morningbell
in 3 minutes

Morningbell is a daily report on US and Taiwan stocks. Each day it sums up what happened, where the market stands, and, in a big drop, whether it is a pullback or a turn to a bear market. You don’t need to read every page. Follow the order below: 1 minute on a normal day, 5 minutes in a big drop.

1

When to read itTwo editions a day. Pick one time.

07:30Morning edition

After the US close. The big number on the front page is the S&P 500. It also says what to watch at the Taiwan open.

16:30Afternoon edition

After the Taiwan close and the foreign-investor flow data. The big number is the TAIEX.

Times are Taipei time (UTC+8).

2

On the front page, check these 3 thingsOn a normal day, you can stop here

  1. 1
    The headline: what happened today

    One line on the most important thing today. Numbers are marked in blue.

    TAIEX falls 1,873 points, loses 40,000…
  2. 2
    Bull or bear: what kind of market this is

    One box each for Taiwan, the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq 100. It tells you if it is an uptrend, a pullback, or a bear market.

    Uptrend intactPullbackBear, risk rising
  3. 3
    Today’s plan

    General rules written in advance. On most days the answer is “keep your regular investing plan, nothing extra to do.”

    DoDon’tWatch
3

Worried in a big drop? Read “Bull or bear”It answers one question: is this a pullback, or a turn to a bear market?

● Systemic risk lights

Warnings that credit, the economy or financial conditions are in trouble. The more that are on, the more it looks like a real bear market.

●3 or more on → be careful, may be turning bear○None on → the economy is not the problem

● Capitulation signals

Signs of panic selling. The more that are on, the closer selling may be to burning out (not a promise of the bottom).

●Oversold, margin calls, heavy foreign selling…

Below that is “After drops like this in the past”: when the market fell this far before, how deep it went and what it did a year later. It usually calms people down. At the end there is always “What would change this view”, with exact price levels.

4

Wondering whether to add? Read the “Focus list”The 9 names the paper tracks: S&P 500, QQQ, 0050 and 6 funds

Each one has a rule reading tag, from the same public rules:

Add zone

Down 5% or more from the high. A tier of the add-on ladder has been hit.

Near an add tier

Within 2% of the next add tier.

Neutral

No rule triggered. Keep regular monthly investing.

Warm

Up a lot: 20% or more above the long-term line. Be careful chasing.

Hot

30% or more above the long-term line. A time to review weights and pause adding.

Tap a card for details: Today’s read (why), Key levels (the price of each add tier and how far away it is), and Related news.

5

−1.2%Green = down

The one thing to remember

Morningbell’s tags and readings are rule-based readings and historical statistics. They are not forecasts and not investment advice. On most days the market just moves normally. Understanding what happened and sticking to your own plan matters more than reacting to every move.
